Explore African Culinary Regions

Africa has many different food traditions. Each region has its own ingredients, spices and cooking methods.

West Africa

West African food is known for its rich flavours and spicy dishes. Common ingredients include tomatoes, rice, onions and peppers.

One popular dish is Jollof Rice, which is enjoyed in many West African countries.

Explore Jollof Rice

East Africa

East African cuisine includes vegetables, grains, meat and traditional spices. Different communities have their own food traditions.

Popular dishes include Isombe from Rwanda and Injera from Ethiopia.

Explore Isombe
Explore Injera

North Africa

North African cuisine is influenced by Mediterranean and Arab food traditions. Common ingredients include cumin, cinnamon, olive oil and other spices.

A famous dish is Moroccan Tagine, which is slowly cooked with vegetables and spices.
