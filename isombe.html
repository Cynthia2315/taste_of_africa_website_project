Isombe

About Isombe

Isombe is a traditional food item of the Rwandans which is prepared from Cassava. leaves. It is prepared with peanut butter, and is an excellent source of protein. onions and spices. It's a significant component of Rwandan food culture.

Ingredients

  • Cassava leaves
  • Peanut butter
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Spices

How to Prepare

Show cooking steps

Wash and crush leaves of cassava.

Step 2: Add spices and onions to the leaves and cook.

3. Add peanut butter and cook again.
