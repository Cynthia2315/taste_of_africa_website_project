Traditional Ethiopian Injera

Injera is a traditional Ethiopian flatbread. It is soft, sour and commonly served with different stews.

Ingredients

  • Teff flour
  • Water
  • A little salt

Cooking Instructions

Show steps
  1. Mix the flour and water.
  2. Leave the mixture to ferment.
  3. Heat a non-stick pan.
  4. Pour in the batter and cook until bubbles appear and the surface is set.
  5. Remove and serve with stew.
